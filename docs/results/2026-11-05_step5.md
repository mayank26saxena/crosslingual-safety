# Step 5 results: selective neurons and longer replies (Llama)

Pre-registration: [2026-11-05_selective_neurons.md](../prereg/2026-11-05_selective_neurons.md). Aggregate outputs are in `results/cue_tmt_llama/`. Raw
replies are not tracked.

## Summary

**The selective neurons pass the pre-registered rule on Llama.** With no cue in the prompt, refusal on held-out harmful Bengali requests rises from 31.6% to 59.6%, and answers to
harmless Bengali prompts fall only from 94.9% to 86.9%, inside the 90% limit. This is a result about selective refusal on one model, from an instruction-derived source. It is not a safety claim.

## Selection on development data

Both source halves selected λ = 1, α = 1, the same setting. Selection scores are optimistic and are not results. The grid shows the penalty controlling the trade-off:

| Half A source | α 0.5: refusal / answering | α 1: refusal / answering | α 2: refusal / answering |
|---|---|---|---|
| λ 0.3 | 68.9 / 75.0 | 95.9 / 18.3 | 98.6 / 0.0 |
| λ 1 | 58.1 / 88.3 | **67.6 / 85.8** | 78.4 / 68.3 |
| λ 3 | 14.9 / 94.2 | 5.4 / 95.8 | 4.1 / 95.8 |
| λ 10 | 10.8 / 95.8 | 1.4 / 96.7 | 0.0 / 97.5 |

At higher penalties refusal falls below the unchanged 32.7%, so the filter then selects neurons that favour answering. That was not predicted.

## Held-out test

Harmful requests use three replies of up to 400 new tokens, pooled over both directions.

| Condition | Refusal, harmful | Answers harmless |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unchanged | 31.6% | 94.9% |
| Random neurons | 32.0% | 93.1% |
| **Selected neurons** | **59.6%** | **86.9%** |

| Comparison | Gain [95% interval] | Half A | Half B |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Over unchanged | +28.0 [+21.8, +34.0] | +32.4 | +23.7 |
| Over random | +27.6 [+21.5, +33.8] | +27.5 | +27.6 |

| Registered check | Result | Limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Refusal kappa | 0.792 | At least 0.6 |
| Answering relative to unchanged | 91.6% | At least 90% |
| Bengali-script replies | 100% | At least 90% |
| Repeated or digit-heavy replies | 0.9% | At most 10% |
| Blind check: garbled selected replies | 0 of 40 | At most 10% |

Long replies fix the length problem from Step 4: mean length is about 300 characters.

## Harm, provisional

Unsafe replies under `harm_response_type` fall from 26.4% unchanged, and 28.2% random, to 16.2% selected. Judge kappa on this measure is 0.377, below the 0.6 threshold, so this is provisional and is not evidence of lower harm.

## Limits

- **Refusal-then-help:** in the blind read of 60 replies, 4 of 40 selected and 1 of 20 unchanged replies decline and then give related help, so some counted refusals may not be protective. This count was not a registered measure.
- **Harmful answers remain:** 40% of selected replies do not refuse. Bengali remains below the English unchanged rate of 75.5%.
- **The margin on help is small,** and it has no interval in the rule. The help drop is about 8 points.
- **XSTest was used to fit the filter.** Half B is held out, but it comes from the same benchmark, and its unfiltered results were seen before this step. No other benchmark tested this.
- **The source is an explicit instruction cue,** so these neurons may reflect instruction-following.
- **No comparison was run against unfiltered neurons at low strength** or the reminder's own help rate. The result does not show that the filter beats those.
- One model, 150 requests, no human or native review, and the blind check was graded by one session. Gate E1 and the D1 NO-GO stand. The sealed split was not read.
