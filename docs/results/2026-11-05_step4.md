# Step 4 results: cue-based neuron transfer on MultiJail (Llama)

Pre-registration: [2026-11-05_cue_transfer.md](../prereg/2026-11-05_cue_transfer.md). Aggregate outputs are in `results/cue_tmt_llama/`.
Raw replies are not tracked.

## Summary

**The registered result is a failure of the help check.** Neurons found from a Bengali safety reminder raise refusal on held-out plain
Bengali requests, but they also make the model refuse harmless prompts. No selective refusal mechanism was found, the shared-or-specific
question cannot be read on Llama, and no safety claim follows.

## Stage 0: does a cue raise refusal?

150 dev requests, five sampled replies each. Judge kappa is 0.831 on Bengali and 0.932 on English.

| Prompt | Cue | Refusal | Usable pairs, half A / half B |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Bengali | None | 31.7% | |
| Bengali | Neutral test notice, Bengali | 30.9% | 5 / 7 |
| Bengali | Neutral test notice, English | 30.9% | 5 / 4 |
| Bengali | Safety reminder, Bengali | 53.1% | 25 / 24 |
| English | None | 75.5% | |
| English | Neutral test notice | 80.7% | 7 / 5 |
| English | Safety reminder | 84.9% | 9 / 9 |

A neutral test notice does not raise Bengali refusal on Llama, so the study went forward with the safety reminder, an explicit instruction
cue. The English source fell below the 15-pair rule, so the transfer comparison cannot be read.

## Stages 1-2: steering plain Bengali requests

Neurons from one half steer the other half. Harmless answering is from XSTest half B with half-A neurons. Judge kappa on steered replies is
0.753 on Bengali and 0.773 on English.

| Condition | Refusal, harmful | Gain over random [95% interval] | Answers harmless |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Unchanged | 31.7% | | 94.9% |
| Random neurons ×4 | 31.0% | | 94.4% |
| Reminder neurons ×2 | 99.3% | +68.3 [+62.7, +73.6] | 0.5% |
| Reminder neurons ×4 | 82.0% | +51.0 [+42.7, +59.7] | 5.6% |
| English neurons ×2, exploratory | 93.6% | +62.5 [+55.7, +69.2] | 1.8% |
| English neurons ×4, exploratory | 61.6% | +30.5 [+21.5, +38.8] | 13.6% |

**Verdict:** the reminder neurons fail at both strengths on the 90% help rule. Under the registered continuation rule, Qwen and Gemma do not
run. English neurons came from 9 pairs per half, and are not part of any verdict.

**Overlap of the top 5,000 neurons:** chance is about 55. English and Bengali sources share 763 and 986. Halves of one source share 2,796
(Bengali) and 2,175 (English).

**Reading, exploratory:** these neurons act like a refusal control that does not depend on harm, and their effect crosses language.
Language-specific recognition is not needed to trigger them.

## Reply quality

The automatic reply check reports 100% usable for the reminder neurons at ×4, but it is too weak to rely on. In the blind sample below, 17
of 40 ×4 replies were graded garbled on the Bengali pass, while the main judge marked 33 of those 40 as refusals. So the ×4 refusal rate
partly counts broken text. An unblinded spot check of ×2 replies finds coherent Bengali refusals, with similar wording on harmful and harmless
prompts. Some English-source ×2 replies switch to English.

## Frontier-model grading check

Claude Opus 5.5 graded 100 blinded Bengali replies twice, directly and through a Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct English translation, in a different
order under separate ids. Grades were refusal, other answer, apparent harmful help, or garbled.

| Agreement between passes | Kappa | Raw agreement |
|---|---:|---:|
| All four grades | 0.688 | 79% |
| Refusal or not | 0.693 | 85% |
| Harmful help or not | 0.592 | 94% |

Refusal agreement with the main judge on the Bengali pass is kappa 0.647. Harmful-help agreement misses the 0.6 threshold, on few positive
cases. This is a consistency check, because one session graded both passes and could recognise replies, and it has no human reference. It does
not establish a harmful-reply measure.

## A limit on Llama replies

Generation stops at 100 new tokens. On Llama that gives a mean of 82 characters in Bengali, against 194 in English, and 41.9% of plain Bengali
replies stop inside a character. Many replies stop before any substantive content, so harm cannot be judged from them. The same token limit
applied to the Step 3 Llama replies. A harmful-reply measure on Bengali needs a much longer limit.

## Limits

- One model, one instruction cue, and 150 requests. No human or native review. The Bengali cues and XSTest translations are Claude Opus 5.5
  translations.
- Refusal is not safety. Earlier steps found that refusal and judged harm do not move together on Bengali.
- The sealed split was not read.

## What would settle more

- Longer replies on Bengali, with human or stronger grading of harm.
- Neuron selection penalised on harmless requests, as TMT's selective variant did.
- Steering gated on a harm detector, evaluated on separate benchmarks.
